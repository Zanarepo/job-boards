🚀 Remote Job Search Tips

🎯 Optimize for Keywords

Remote companies use ATS. Ensure your CV mentions specific tools like Slack, Zoom, Jira, and Asana.

🌍 Timezone Overlap

Applying to US/Europe from Africa/Asia? State clearly: "Willing to overlap 4+ hours with EST/CET timezones."

📎 Proof of Work

Links to GitHub, Behance, or a personal blog are worth 1000x more than a degree in the remote world.

🤝 Networking is Key

Don't just apply. Find the hiring manager on LinkedIn and send a polite, 2-sentence note about your interest.
